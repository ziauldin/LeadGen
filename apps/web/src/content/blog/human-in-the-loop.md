It's now trivial to generate thousands of "personalised" emails. That's exactly why most of them get ignored. Recipients can tell when nobody thought about them before the email was sent.

## What AI is good at

Given a clear brief (what you sell, who it's for, the tone you want) and a few facts about the lead, a language model can write a reasonable first draft quickly. That saves real time, especially on follow-ups.

## What it gets wrong

It invents details. It overstates. It sometimes writes something technically personal but obviously generic. And it has no sense of whether this particular person should be emailed at all.

## How LeadsGen handles it

- **AI is optional** and uses your own provider key.
- **Every draft waits for approval.** The approval queue is designed to be fast (keyboard shortcuts, bulk actions) so reviewing doesn't become a bottleneck.
- **Compliance checks run on every draft**, so a person approving can see straight away if something's missing.
- **Edits are tracked**, so you can see what was written by AI and what was changed by a person.

The result is fewer, better emails, and replies from people who are glad you got in touch.
