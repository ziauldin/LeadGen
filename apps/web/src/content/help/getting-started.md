## 1. Fill in your sender profile

Go to **Settings → Workspace** and add your name, company, business address and website. These appear in every email, and the business address is a legal requirement in many countries.

## 2. Connect a sending account

Open **Settings → Providers** and connect Resend, SendGrid, Mailgun or your own SMTP server. Each workspace uses its own account, so your reputation is never shared with anyone else. See [Connect a sending provider](/help/sending-provider).

## 3. Check deliverability

**Settings → Deliverability** checks SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your sending domain. Fix anything flagged before you send. See [Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC](/help/deliverability).

## 4. Add leads

Create a niche (for example "Dental practices in Leeds"), then either search public listings, import a CSV or add leads by hand. Every lead needs a source and a note on why you're contacting them.

## 5. Build a campaign

Choose **New campaign**, pick your niche and write one or more steps. Use variables like `{{first_name}}` and `{{company}}`. The unsubscribe link is added for you.

## 6. Review and send

Adding leads creates drafts in the **Approvals** queue. Review them (keyboard shortcuts make this quick), approve the good ones and start the campaign. Emails go out within your sending window and daily limits.
