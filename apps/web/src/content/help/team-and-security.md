## Roles

- **Owner**: everything, including billing.
- **Admin**: everything except billing.
- **Member**: find leads, write and approve emails, run campaigns.

Enterprise workspaces can create **custom roles** with exactly the permissions people need, under **Settings → Roles**.

## Two-factor authentication

Turn it on under **Settings → Security** with any authenticator app. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe. Owners can require two-factor for everyone in the workspace.

## Single sign-on

Pro and Enterprise workspaces can sign in with their own identity provider (Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace and others). Verify your domain, add the provider and optionally require SSO. See **Settings → Single sign-on**.

## Audit log

**Settings → Audit log** shows who changed what and when. You can filter and export it.
