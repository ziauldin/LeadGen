When you add leads to a campaign, LeadsGen writes a draft for each one and puts it in **Approvals**. Nothing is sent until someone approves it.

## What's checked automatically

Each draft shows live compliance checks:

- the lead has a source and a lawful basis note
- the recipient isn't on the suppression list
- the email includes an unsubscribe link and your business address
- the lead hasn't opted out or been disqualified

Drafts with a problem can't be approved until it's fixed.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- `j` / `k`: next and previous draft
- `a`: approve
- `r`: reject
- `e`: edit
- `?`: show all shortcuts

## Rewriting a draft

Use **Rewrite from template** to start again from a template, or **Write with AI** to get a fresh draft from your instructions. The new version still waits for approval.

## Who can approve

Anyone with the *Approve and reject emails* permission. Members have it by default; Enterprise workspaces can create custom roles without it.
