Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, people can ask what you hold about them and ask you to delete it. Your workspace is the controller of that data, so these requests come to you.

## Export a person's data

1. Go to **Settings → Privacy**.
2. Enter their email address and click **Export their data**.
3. When it's ready, download the zip and send it to them securely.

The export includes their contact details, lead profile, the emails you sent, their replies and any do-not-contact entries.

## Erase a person

Click **Erase this person** and confirm. LeadsGen deletes their leads, contact details, emails, replies and campaign history. Only a one-way hash of their address is kept so they're never contacted again.

## Retention rules

Under **Data governance** you can automatically delete leads you never emailed, and replies older than a set period.

## Lawful basis

Each lead records why you may contact them: legitimate interest, consent or contract. Workspaces can require a short legitimate-interest note before any outreach.
