## Your plan and usage

**Settings → Plan & usage** shows your plan, what you've used this month and your limits.

## Upgrading

Workspace owners can choose a plan, monthly or yearly, and pay securely with Stripe. The new limits apply as soon as payment is confirmed.

## Changing or cancelling

Click **Manage billing** to change plan, update your card, download invoices or cancel. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a free trial of the Starter plan. When it ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan unless you've chosen a paid plan. Nothing is deleted.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails you'll see a banner. Update your card within the grace period to avoid sending being paused. Everything else keeps working.
